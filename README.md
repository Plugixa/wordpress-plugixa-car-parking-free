## What it is

🅿️ Parking reservations on your own site, free and with no trial or expiry. Records live in the plugin's own database tables, not in posts and post meta, so the bookings list, the calendar and the dashboard stay fast as your history grows:

- **Booking form** - dates, space, details and confirmation, placed with the `[plugixa_car_parking]` shortcode or a Gutenberg block, in three designs (Classic, Split and Minimal)
- **Live availability** - each space type shows how many spaces are left for the dates chosen, and availability is checked again on the server so a space is never sold twice
- **Server-side pricing** - prices per minute, hour, day, week or month for each space type, worked out on the server and never trusted from the browser
- **Locations** - any number of car parks, each with opening hours, closures and special days, and a setup check that lists what is missing before it can be booked
- **Managing bookings** - a dashboard for the last 30 days, a bookings list with filters and CSV export, bookings taken in the admin, a calendar by month, week and day, and pending bookings that expire and free their space
- **Payments** - pay on arrival with your own instructions, and a payments ledger to record payments and refunds against bookings
- **Customers** - coupon codes, your own fields on the booking form, a booking summary page with an invoice (`[plugixa_car_parking_summary]`) and an account page to find and cancel a booking (`[plugixa_car_parking_account]`)
- **For developers and teams** - a REST API, actions and filters, and six capabilities to grant with a role editor; translation-ready, with right-to-left support and a dark mode on the booking pages

Card payments through **Stripe, PayPal, Mollie, Adyen and Klarna**, pricing rules, numbered spaces, extras, text messages, reports, check-in with QR codes and season passes are part of Plugixa Car Parking Pro. A full tour is on the [documentation](https://www.plugixa.com/documentation/plugixa-car-parking?utm_source=github.com&utm_medium=repo_readme&utm_campaign=plugixa-car-parking&utm_content=docs) - and on the [WordPress.org plugin page](https://wordpress.org/plugins/plugixa-car-parking/).

## Who is it for?

Parking lots and garages taking reservations online. Airport park & ride and valet services. Hospital and hotel parking. Residential and EV-charging spaces rented by the hour, the day or the month.

## Get it

The free plugin itself is hosted on WordPress.org - install it from your dashboard (**Plugins → Add New → search "Plugixa Car Parking"**) or fetch it directly:

```
Latest release:  https://downloads.wordpress.org/plugin/plugixa-car-parking.latest-stable.zip
Install by ZIP:  Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin → choose the ZIP → Activate
Requires:        WordPress 6.5+, PHP 8.2+
```
